Frequently asked questions

Do I need a deposit to reserve a car?
No — you can book a free test drive with no deposit. A refundable holding fee only applies if you'd like us to pause a listing while you arrange finance.
Can I trade in a car that still has finance owing?
Yes. We'll settle any outstanding finance directly with your lender as part of the valuation and paperwork.
How quickly can finance be approved?
Most applications get a decision within 48 hours, and many are approved the same day.
